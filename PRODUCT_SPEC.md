# Product specification\n\n**Product:** Retirement Reality Check\n**Brand:** Wealth With Aviral\n**Language:** English\n\n## Journey\n1. Retirement intention, age and ideal-life reflection.\n2. Income, expenses, corpus, monthly investments, other income, one-time costs, dependents and insurance.\n3. Inflation, pre/post-retirement return, safety buffer, preferred work style and concerns.\n4. Required corpus, projected corpus, gap/surplus, future expenses, indicative contribution and three scenarios.\n5. Explicit consent then AI-generated explanation, strengths, risks, reflection questions, 30-day actions and caveats.\n\n## Guardrails\nFinancial calculations are deterministic code. AI explains only; it must not change numbers, promise returns or recommend products/securities. No user data is persisted in v1. This is educational information, not financial advice. Lifestyle answers are not a validated psychological score.\n
